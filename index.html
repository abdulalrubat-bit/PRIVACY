Privacy Policy — Warehouse Empire

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Overview

Warehouse Empire is an offline idle game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data collection

The app does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Game saves

Your game progress is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently.

Third-party services

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts to display text. When this occurs, your device makes a standard network request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address as with any web request. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. No other third-party services are used.

Advertising

The app currently contains no advertising. If advertising is added in a future update, this policy will be updated before release.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
